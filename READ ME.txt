Controls:

Up Arrow	= Move up
Down Arrow	= Move down
Left Arrow	= Move left
Right Arrow 	= Move right

Space Bar	= Interact/Select

Esc		= Pause/Menu/Back

R		= Resets player to map entrance to prevent soft lock.


(Debug shortcuts)

R = Reset to entrance

B = Teleport to dungeon1 map
N = Teleport to dungeon2 map
